Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

This policy explains what Wax Ledger collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account and billing: if you subscribe, Stripe collects your name, email and payment details. We receive your email, plan and payment status, not your full card number.
  • Newsletter: your email address if you sign up.
  • Searches: card searches are stored, without your identity, to build and refresh the site's price records and to count what people look up.
  • Basic logs: our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and reliability.

What we don't collect

Photos you add to the Pre-Grading Tool are processed in your browser and never uploaded to us.

How we use it

To run the site, process your membership, send the newsletter and account emails, and improve the tools. We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site: Stripe (payments), Netlify (hosting), our email provider, and data providers that answer card price lookups (which receive search terms, not who searched).

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, manage or cancel your membership from the billing page, and email us to see or delete the personal information we hold about you.

Cookies and storage

The site saves a few settings in your browser (like your last tab and grading assumptions). We don't use advertising trackers.
