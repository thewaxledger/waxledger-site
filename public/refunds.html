Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated October 8, 2026

Cancel any time

You can cancel your membership whenever you like from the manage subscription page (sign in with the email you subscribed with), or by emailing info@thewaxledger.com. Your membership stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Refunds

If you're not happy within 7 days of your first payment, email us and we'll refund it in full. After that, payments are non-refundable, but cancelling stops all future charges. If you were charged in error, contact us and we'll make it right.

Annual plans

Annual memberships can be refunded in full within 7 days of purchase or renewal. After that, cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the year ends.

Questions: info@thewaxledger.com
