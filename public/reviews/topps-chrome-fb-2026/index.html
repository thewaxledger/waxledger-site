Written Nov 16 and re-priced Nov 17, the day before release (Nov 18): the box figures are now eBay presale sales; every single is still pending (the FDI jumbo boxes shipped Nov 13, but only one FDI single, a Lenoir Molten Mercury 1/6 at $40, has a completed sale). Chrome Football is the one football product that gets graded by the thousand, and this year's comes seven months after the last one: the 2025 set did not ship until Apr 15, 2026, so the Mendoza, Simpson and Love Refractors land in a market that still has 2025's Ward and Hunter Refractors fresh on it. The box is $439.99 at Topps, up $90 on 2025, for 80 cards and one autograph; the street is paying $575 (median of 40 presale boxes sold on eBay Nov 12 to 16, 36 of them between $559 and $600) before a single hobby box has been opened, which is where the 2025 box has been trading on StockX all autumn ($588 average on 13 sales, from $349.99 at release). The case for the box is the same every year: the rookie Refractor ladder (Lavender /299 down to Red /5, with Lava versions) and a 100-rookie base run are the cards that get sent to PSA, and a Mendoza Gold /50 or Orange /25 out of a hobby box is a four-figure card on the Inception and Midnight evidence ($2,000 Gold /50, $800 Red /99, $950 Equinox /20). The case against it is arithmetic: one autograph in 20 packs from 15 autograph sets, 92 of them Rookie Variation Autographs and 106 of them debut patches, in a month where Mendoza autographs already trade in Inception and Midnight and Chrome adds a 92-card rookie set on top. The hobby box pays off on a numbered rookie Refractor, not the autograph. Until the first cases are broken the numbers to fill in are whether the $575 presale box holds through release week, and where a plain Mendoza Rookie Variation Autograph opens.

Tier 1 · The cards worth opening a box for

  • Fernando Mendoza #351 RC · Refractor, Gold /50, Orange /25, Black /10, Red /5 and the Rookie Variation AutographPrices pending (release Nov 18; FDI singles shipped Nov 13 but were not read). Comp: Inception Rookie Autograph Red /99 $800 (Nov 7), Gold Electricity /50 $2,000 (Nov 8); Midnight Equinox autograph /20 $950 (Nov 13), Rookie Refractor SP #21 $102.64 (Nov 14)

    The first pick and the card the set is bought for. His Chrome base Refractor is the card that will be graded most this winter; his numbered colours are where the Inception and Midnight money went ($800 to $2,000 for /99 and /50 autographs). A Rookie Variation Autograph of his is one card in a 92-card set with no odds published, so the single will cost less than the box in week one, as it did in both earlier products.

  • Ty Simpson #357, Jeremiyah Love #302, Carson Beck #301 · Refractors and Rookie Variation AutographsPrices pending. Comp: Simpson Inception autographs $150 to $300 (6 sales Nov 6 to 9); Love Midnight Rookie Autograph /125 $225 and Midnight Oil Marks $140 (Nov 13 to 14); Beck Midnight patch autograph $127.50 and Rookie Autograph Midnight $75

    The second tier: the Rams quarterback, the one running back with a market and the Cardinals' other quarterback. Inception and Midnight put Simpson at $150 to $300 and Love at $140 to $225 for autographs; Chrome's plain autographs should open there and the numbered Refractors (Gold /50 and shorter) are the ones that hold through the season.

  • 2023 and 2024 Prem1ere Patch Autographs (47 and 59) · the NFL debut patches, new to ChromePrices pending

    The NFL debut patches from the 2023 and 2024 classes, 106 autographs with a game-worn debut patch each, moved into Chrome packs this year. The names are one and two classes old and the patch is the real thing, which is more than any other autograph in the box offers; which box they fall in is a breaker's question until the odds sheet is up.

  • Tecmo Autographs 14 /10, Chromographs 24 /50, Gold Shield 5 1/1 · the short-numbered veteran and legend autographsPrices pending

    The autographs that hold a year on: short-numbered veterans and Hall of Famers. Fourteen Tecmo cards numbered to 10 and 24 Chromographs to 50 are the case hits of the autograph checklist; everything else in the autograph slots runs to 1/1 from /499.

Tier 2 · Mid-range: the numbered Refractors and the big insert sets

  • Rookie numbered Refractors · Lavender /299 to Green /99, and the Lava versionsPrices pending

    The grading product's middle: a rookie Green /99 or Blue /150 of a quarterback is the card that pays for the box when the autograph does not. Lavender /299, Yellow /275 and Pink /250 are three extra tiers on last year's ladder, which spreads the same demand across more cards; expect the /299 to /199 colours to open under the 2025 equivalents.

  • Veteran numbered Refractors and Chrome Etch Variations (50) · Gold /50 and shorter of the quarterbacksPrices pending

    Veteran Gold /50 and Orange /25 of Mahomes, Allen, Burrow, Jackson and Daniels are the only veteran base cards worth more than a grading fee; Chrome Etch is the new variation set with no odds, so its scarcity is unknown until breakers post pulls.

  • Rookie Variation Autographs (92) and Base Variation Autographs (81) · the one-a-box autograph, /499 to 1/1Prices pending

    The commonest autograph in the box and 173 cards wide; outside the top eight or nine rookies these are $15 to $60 cards on the 2025 pattern, and the /499 run means the base version is not scarce. Buy the numbered colours of the names you want, not the set.

  • Instinct (40), Destiny (35), Future Stars (25), Radiating Rookies (20), Helix (15), Kaiju (10) · the rookie insertsPrices pending

    Six rookie-heavy insert sets, two of them new (Destiny, Instinct). Unnumbered inserts of Mendoza and Simpson are $5 to $20 cards in week one and their Gold /50 and shorter colours are the ones that get graded.

Tier 3 · Everything else in the box

  • Base cards · 400 of them, 300 veterans, 100 rookies, five legends; Refractors unnumberedPrices pending

    Most of the 80 cards in the box. Veteran base is a dollar card; rookie base of the top names $3 to $10; the unnumbered Refractor is the one worth grading when it is Mendoza, Simpson or Love and centred. The 2025 set's 400 cards are the same shape and the same prices a year on.

  • Ball of Duty, Game Genies, Glass Canvas, Gridlock, Power Players, All Chrome Team, 1991 Topps, Lightning Leaders · the veteran insertsPrices pending

    The inserts that fill the non-Refractor slots; $2 to $8 unnumbered, with the Madden 99 Club /99 and the 2016 to 2018 Topps Chrome throwbacks the only ones above that. Lombardi Trophy is a 1/1 and a lottery ticket, not a reason.

  • Retail formats · Mega (Dec 2), Value blaster, Hanger; Retail Rookie Autographs (43)Prices pending

    Retail gets its own autograph set and its own colours (X-Fractor, Ray Wave, Football Leather, Pulsar) at $20 to $80 a box; the mega ships with the seal removed, which makes it a rip-it product by design. Collect the Pulsars and X-Fractors of the top rookies as singles; the boxes are for opening.

What to skip

  • Paying $575 or more for a box before the first cases are broken (40 presale boxes sold at that median Nov 12 to 16). The 2025 box ($349.99) settled at a $588 StockX average seven months on; the 2026 box starts $90 higher and the asking prices already assume it holds the same premium.

  • Buying a box as a way to get a Mendoza autograph. He is one card in a 92-card Rookie Variation Autograph set with no odds published, in his third autograph product this month; the single will cost less than the box in week one, as his Inception and Midnight autographs did.

  • Reading the FDI jumbo's $2,500 into the regular jumbo at $789.99. FDI carries a Molten Mercury autograph and two Molten Mercury parallels on top of the three autographs; the regular jumbo has two autographs and none of them.

  • Treating a Lavender /299, Yellow /275 or Pink /250 as a short print. Three new tiers above /199 mean a numbered Refractor is no longer rare on its own; the price is in Gold /50 and shorter.

  • Buying the mega box as sealed wax. It ships from Topps.com with the seal removed by design and will not hold value as a sealed product.
