2026 Topps Heritage Football

5.0WAX SCORE / 10
  • Hobby: 20 packs of 8 (160 cards) with one autograph or relic per box, 12 boxes a case. $129.99 at Topps. eBay sold since release: $186 median (46 boxes, $160 to $255, Oct 20 to 22); presale boxes went $190 median (117 since Sep 29)
  • Mega box: 17 packs of 8, $49.99, ships Nov 4; presale sales $75 median (21, $60 to $96, Sep 29 to Oct 22). Value blaster: 8 packs of 8, $24.99
  • 400-card base set on the 1976 Topps design, the first Heritage Football since the 2015 set. 100 rookies marked RC: Mendoza #272, Jeremiyah Love #133, Ty Simpson #288, Carnell Tate #305, Makai Lemon #18, Drew Allar #27, Caleb Downs #187
  • A 400-card Chrome parallel set runs alongside the paper set, Refractor through SuperFractor 1/1. 150 image variations (alternate jersey, black and white, flipped helmet, legend, no-name)
  • Topps has not published pack odds, short-print numbers or a print run; the checklist is marked subject to change
Rookie class7.5Hit quality4.0Print run5.0Price vs. value3.5

Written Oct 20 and refreshed Oct 22, the second day of release, with eBay sold listings. The checklist and box contents are published; pack odds, short prints and the print run are not, so nothing below is stated as a ratio. Prices are eBay completed sales with counts and dates; the first two days of a release are the high-water mark for singles, so expect most of these to drift down. The short version: a hobby box is 160 cards on the 1976 design with one hit that is as often a relic as an autograph, in a set with 100 rookies and a Chrome set riding alongside the paper one. The box sells for $186 on eBay against $129.99 at Topps. The Mendoza paper rookie is a $4 card, the Chrome Refractor a $25 card, and the hits that have sold so far run $9 to $70 for relics and $20 to $199 for autographs. At $129.99 it is a set-builder's box; at $186 the hit has to be an autograph of a name to get the money back, and most are not.

Tier 1 · The cards worth opening a hobby box for

  • Fernando Mendoza · #272 RC paper, Chrome #272, Black Border, Heritage Rookie Autograph, Real One Rookie AutographPaper #272 $3.99 median (22 sales, $2.34 to $5.49 for plain base, Oct 21 to 22). Chrome #272 $25 median (6, $10 to $100, Oct 21). Black Border $25 to $100 (3). No autograph sold yet. For scale, his 2026 Bowman Chrome Rookie Auto sells at $560 (23 sales, $500 to $810, Sep 30 to Oct 9)

    The No. 1 pick, backing up Kirk Cousins in Las Vegas; he has thrown eight NFL passes (6 of 8 for 48 yards in a 45-17 loss to New England on Oct 11). The paper #272 is the card most boxes produce, and it is a $4 card on day two. One autograph or relic per hobby box across a signer list that mixes 100 rookies with veterans and retired players, so a Mendoza autograph in a given box is a long shot; Topps has not said how long, and none has sold.

  • Jeremiyah Love · #133 RC, Ty Simpson #288, Carnell Tate #305, Garrett Nussmeier, Jordyn Tyson · rookie autographsLove paper #133 $1.49 to $1.50 (2 sales, Oct 21); Love image variation $69 (1, Oct 22); Love Rookie Lineage relic $29.99 (1, Oct 22). No Love autograph sold yet

    Cardsmiths Breaks lists Love and Nussmeier with Mendoza as the chase rookie autographs, and Bo Nix and Caleb Williams as the chase veterans. Heritage Rookie Autographs carry at least a Gold /50 parallel (Topps' own teaser). The Love paper rookie at $1.50 says where the non-Mendoza base sits.

  • Low-numbered Chrome parallels · Gold Refractor /50, Orange /25, Red /5, SuperFractor 1/1; paper Color of the Year /76 and Bicentennial /200Chrome Orange /25 De'Zhaun Sterling $65 (1, Oct 22). No Gold, Red or SuperFractor sold yet

    The Chrome set is a full 400 cards, so a numbered Chrome of one particular rookie is one card in 400 at each level. The Bicentennial /200 and Color of the Year /76 are the paper set's numbered parallels, the 76 a nod to the design year.

  • Walter Payton cut autographs; Founding Era Ink; Classic Renditions AutographsNo cut autograph sold yet. The Payton Legend image variation sells at $60 to $85 (3 sales, Oct 21 to 22)

    Steel City's product sheet lists Payton cut signatures. The 1976 Topps #148 Payton rookie is the design's best-known card, and the packaging references it. Expect these at case-hit rarity or rarer; Topps has not published a count.

Tier 2 · Mid-range: variations, Chrome and inserts

  • Image variations · 150 cards: Alternate Jersey (20), Black & White (30), Flipped Helmet (20), Image (50), Legend and No Name (30 between them)$60 median (17 sales, $15 to $175, Oct 18 to 22). Josh Allen #73 $175, Caleb Williams $150, Payton Legend $60 to $85, Drake Maye no-helmet $60, Love $69; Baker Mayfield, Travis Hunter and C.J. Stroud $15 to $20

    Heritage's traditional short-printed chase and, on the first two days, the best-selling non-hit in the box. Topps has not published the football odds, so how many land in a hobby box is unknown. The spread is wide: the quarterbacks of the big-market teams carry it, and the rest sell for the price of a Chrome Refractor. The Black & White list includes a Tom Brady #7 that does not match the base #7 (Trey McBride), one of several checklist inconsistencies Checklist Insider flags.

  • Chrome Refractors and the format sparkles · Light Blue Sparkle (hobby), Silver Sparkle (mega), Pink Sparkle (blaster), Aqua Sparkle (fat pack); Blue /150, Green /99, Black /76Chrome Refractors $5.99 median (50 sales, $1.39 to $160, Oct 20 to 22); Josh Allen Refractor $15, Joe Burrow $5, Christian McCaffrey $6. Mendoza Chrome $25 median (6)

    Each format has its own Chrome sparkle parallel, which splits the chase four ways and keeps the hobby version from being the only one. Blue /150 to Black /76 is the higher-numbered half of the ladder. Outside Mendoza, a Refractor of a star is a $5 to $15 card.

  • Inserts · The Expansion (one star per 1976 franchise, 28 cards), New Age Performers (chromium), All-Pro Series (1976 Wonder Bread design), Sweetness (3 greats, 5 current backs), Through the YearsNew Age Performers Love $6.95 (1, Oct 21). Too few sales of the others to quote

    No odds published. The Expansion marks the Seahawks' and Buccaneers' 1976 arrival; Justin Herbert is the preview card. Through the Years is a Brady set that includes his one-game 2008 season.

  • Relics · Gridiron Collection, Locker Room, Real One, Rookie Lineage; autograph relics in the same four families plus Flashbacks and BicentennialNon-autograph relics $15 median (14 sales, $9 to $70, Oct 20 to 22): Mendoza Gridiron Collection $70, Drew Allar Gridiron $40, Caleb Williams Locker Room $30, Makai Lemon $15, Ty Simpson $13, Cade Klubnik Rookie Lineage $9

    The hobby guarantee is one autograph or relic, and Topps has not said in what proportion. The relic sales so far say what a relic box returns: $9 to $40 unless the swatch is Mendoza's, against a $186 box. That is the usual pattern for Heritage relics. Mega and blaster boxes have no hit guarantee listed.

Tier 3 · Everything else in the box

  • Base cards · 400, with 2025 Record Breakers #1 to 8, League Leaders #201 to 208, team cards #351 to 382, AFC/NFC Championship and Super Bowl LX #331 to 333Love #133 $1.50 (2); 'you pick' base lots list at $0.99 a card. Mendoza #272 is the exception at $3.99

    160 cards a hobby box against a 400-card set: three boxes do not complete the set once the duplicates are counted, and any base short prints (Topps has not said whether the 400 includes them) push that further. 100 of the 400 are rookies, a quarter of the set.

  • Border parallels by format · Dark Gray (hobby), Red (mega), Dark Green (blaster), Dark Yellow (fat pack); Flip Stock and Black Border (hobby)Mendoza Black Border $25 to $100 (3 sales, Oct 21 to 22); Mendoza Green Border $10.99 (1); Love Pink Sparkle $5 to $13 (2)

    Unnumbered. Heritage's border parallels sell for a few dollars outside the top rookies; the Black Border is the one that carries a premium, and on Mendoza it is doing so here.

  • Veteran autographs and relics outside the chase namesAutographs $60 median (8 sales, $0.99 to $199, Oct 20 to 22): Brock Bowers Chrome on-card $199, Cam Ward Real One $75, Tory Horton $60, Jack Sawyer Real One $20, Jonah Coleman Real One Rookie $0.99

    Base Chrome Autographs and Real One Autographs cover veterans and retired players as well as rookies. On a one-hit box, the modal hit is one of these, and the sales so far put the typical one at $20 to $75.

What to skip

  • Paying $186 (the eBay median since release) for a box Topps sells for $129.99. That is $1.16 a card for 160 cards and one hit that is as likely a $15 relic as an autograph. Try Topps.com first.

  • Buying a hobby box for a Mendoza autograph. One hit per box, split between autographs and relics, across 100 rookies plus veterans and legends. None has sold yet; buy the single when they do.

  • Paying day-two prices for the paper rookies. Mendoza #272 at $4 and Love #133 at $1.50 are the ceiling, not the floor: hobby cases are still being opened and base supply only grows.

  • Confusing the Chrome set with the paper set when buying singles. Both are numbered 1 to 400 with the same photos; the Chrome Refractor and the paper base of the same card are listed with the same title, and the Mendoza Chrome sales ran $10 to $100 for what sellers called the same card.

  • Assuming the mega box is the same as the hobby box with fewer packs. Mega is 17 packs of 8 with its own Red Border and Silver Sparkle parallels and no hit guarantee, and it ships Nov 4; presale boxes are selling at $75 against $49.99 retail.

Print run in the middle because Topps has not published one. Price vs. value down from the Oct 20 draft now that the box has a sold price ($186) and the singles have theirs. Next refresh: Mendoza and Love autographs once they sell, and whether the box price settles toward $130 or holds.

  • Verdict: at $129.99 from Topps, a reasonable box for someone who wants the 1976 design and a shot at a rookie autograph. At $186 on eBay, pass; the Mendoza paper and Chrome are $4 and $25 singles, and the image variation of your own team's quarterback is $15 to $60.
