2026 Topps Inception Football

Written Nov 8, two days after release (Nov 6), without sold prices: the browser pane that reads eBay and PSA was not available, so every 2026 price here is pending and the numbers are the structure and last year's box. Inception Football is one seven-card pack for $379.99 with one autograph, the NFL license back for the first time since 2015 (the 2024 set had the logos removed), and a 50-card rookie run led by Fernando Mendoza, Ty Simpson and Jeremiyah Love. The case for it is the license and the quarterback class in a brand whose rookie autographs have been the point of the product since 2015. The case against it is arithmetic: last year's box was $249.99 with two autographs and a relic, this year's is $379.99 with one autograph, and the one autograph is a plain Rookie Autograph roughly one box in four (1:4 packs) and a variation one in ten; last year those two sets settled at $6 and $16 medians across the checklist, with the top four quarterbacks carrying the price at $200 to $365 each. The odds sheet reads as about 60,000 hobby boxes if the 1/1 odds are taken at face value, which is not a small run for a one-pack product. Topps.com sold the presale at $369.99 and the release-day allotment by raffle; the eBay street price, and whether Mendoza's autograph opens where Maye's did, are the two numbers to fill in next week.

Tier 1 · The cards worth opening a box for

  • Fernando Mendoza #151 RC · Rookie Autograph, Rookie Autograph Variation, Provenance Patch Autograph, Silver Signings, and the numbered coloursPrices pending. Comp: Drake Maye 2024 Inception Rookie Autograph about $365 raw on 113 sales and $650 in PSA 10; Caleb Williams about $290 on 82 (Hobby Card Index catalog, Oct 3)

    The first pick and the card the box is bought for. Last year's shape is the guide: the top quarterback's plain Rookie Autograph held above $300 for a year while the set's median rookie autograph was $6. Mendoza's Raiders start decides which side of that line he lands on; his Heritage paper base is a $3.99 card (22 sales, Oct 22), so the autograph is the card, not the base.

  • Ty Simpson #152, Jeremiyah Love #158, Carson Beck #155 · Rookie Autographs and patchesPrices pending. Comp: Jayden Daniels and Bo Nix 2024 Rookie Autographs about $200 each on 70-plus sales; Nix Patch Autograph about $340

    The second tier of a quarterback-heavy class plus the one running back with a market. Last year four quarterbacks held $200 or more a year on and everyone else fell to $5 to $80; expect the same split, with Simpson and Love the likeliest to stay on the right side of it.

  • Dual Rookie Autographs · 7 pairings (Simpson/Klare, Allar/Bernard, Lemon/Payton; Beck/Love previewed as Gold /50) and the 8 Dual Rookie Relic Autograph Books (Mendoza/Washington)Prices pending. Comp: 2024 Dual Rookie Relic Autographs $430 median on 38 sales across 5 cards

    The hit that made last year's box: the duals were the only autograph set with a three-figure median across the whole checklist. Gold /50 falls 1:225 packs, so one every 28 boxes or so; this is the single to buy, not chase.

  • Dawn of Greatness Autographs · 20 retired greats (Brady, Manning, Barry Sanders, Rice, Marino, Donald), Gold /50 at 1:95 packsPrices pending. Comp: 2024 Dawn of Greatness $161 median on 67 sales across 15 cards

    The veteran autograph set that holds its price: last year's median was $161 against $6 for the rookies. On-card for most of the names; a Brady or Manning Gold /50 is a case-level card at 1:95 packs.

Tier 2 · Mid-range: the numbered middle and the second autograph sets

  • Provenance Patch Autographs · 43 cards, Aqua /75 at 1:26 packs, Gold Electricity /50 and downPrices pending. Comp: 2024 Provenance Patch Autographs $21.49 median on 312 sales across 35 cards; Maye about $540, Williams $460 (38 sales), DeJean $120 (20)

    The patch autograph that lands in a minority of boxes. The median is a $20 card because the checklist is 43 deep and the patches are player-worn, not game-used (Topps says player-worn and does not claim game use); the top quarterbacks are the exception at $400 and up last year.

  • Charged Particles (12: Allen, Henry, Jacobs, Nacua, Surtain) and Immersion (16: Bowers, Nabers, Warren, Loveland) veteran autographs · Gold /50 at 1:575 and 1:130 packsPrices pending

    New to the football set from Inception Baseball. Veteran autographs in a product built around rookies; Charged Particles at 1:575 packs for the Gold /50 is a one-a-case-and-a-half pull and will price like one. Buy the single of the player you want.

  • Numbered base parallels · Purple /150 (1:3), Red /99 (1:4), Aqua /75 (1:5), Gold Electricity /50 (1:8), Hot Pink /35 (1:11), Blue /25 (1:15), Orange /10 (1:39), Sky /5 (1:77)Prices pending. Comp: 2024 base rookies about $1.60 raw, veterans about $2

    About two numbered cards in the seven, mostly Purple and Red of veterans, which are $2 to $5 cards. Rookie colours run two to three times longer than the veterans' (Purple /150 at 1:8), so a Mendoza Blue /25 is a one-in-many-cases card.

  • Silver Signings (10 rookies, 1:59, with a Gold Ink 1/1 at 1:13,795) and Genesis Autographed Relic Books (24 rookies, 1:74, Purple /25 at 1:110)Prices pending

    The two rookie autograph sets below the duals. Silver Signings is a ten-card set of the class's top names and has carried a premium over the plain Rookie Autograph in past years; Genesis books are big relics with a sticker autograph and sell on the player.

Tier 3 · Everything else in the pack

  • Base cards · four or five of the seven, 150 veterans and 50 rookies, Green parallel at 1:3Prices pending. Comp: 2024 base rookies about $1.60 raw, PSA 10 rookies about $36

    Most of the pack. Inception base is a thick, numbered-feel card with a $1 to $2 market; the only grading case is a Mendoza or Simpson base RC, and last year's PSA 10 rookies at $36 against a $39.99 grading fee say no.

  • Plain Rookie Autographs · 46 cards at 1:4 packs; Rookie Autograph Variations 20 at 1:10Prices pending. Comp: 2024 Rookie Autographs $6.31 median on 1,857 sales across 48 cards; Variations $16.43 on 718 across 19

    The likeliest autograph in the box and, outside the top four or five names, a $5 to $15 card on sticker. This is the number that sets the value of a $380 box: one autograph with a $6 median checklist, carried by whether the name is one of the quarterbacks.

  • Launch inserts (25) and Rookie Short Prints (25, unnumbered Alpha parallels)Prices pending. Comp: 2024 Launch about $9

    The only inserts in the set. Launch is the brand's recurring rookie insert and a $5 to $10 card; the Rookie Short Prints are the same 25 players and will depend on how short they prove once breakers post pull counts.

What to skip

  • Paying $380 for a box as a way to get a Mendoza autograph. Rookie Autographs fall 1:4 packs across 46 names, so the odds of the one you want are long; the single will cost less than the box in week one, as Maye's did last year.

  • Treating this like last year's box. The 2024 product was $249.99 with two autographs and a relic and the logos removed; this one is $379.99 with one autograph and the license. The license is worth something, but not a third hit's worth.

  • Reading the FDI's $900 into the hobby box. FDI boxes carry a /22 patch autograph and a second autograph; the hobby box carries neither.

  • Buying rookie Purple /150 and Red /99 parallels at autograph prices. They are the commonest numbered cards in the set and $3 to $10 cards for everyone but the top three.

  • Trusting a two-autograph description. DraftKings Network wrote two autographs per hobby box; Checklist Insider's odds sheet and Topps' launch page say one. Price the box on one.

  • Verdict for now: a singles product. The license and the class are real and the one card you want is cheaper than the box that might hold it. Buy Mendoza and Simpson Rookie Autographs in the first week while breakers flood the market, as Maye's and Williams's buyers did in 2025; buy the box only if a seven-card rip at $380 is the point. Investors: wait for the box's second-week eBay median before deciding anything, and compare it with the 2024 box, which sits near $510 sealed on a $249.99 start with three hits.
