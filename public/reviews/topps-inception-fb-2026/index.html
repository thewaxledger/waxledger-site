Written Nov 8, re-priced Nov 10 from eBay sold listings through Nov 9. The box trades above Topps: $475 median on 39 single hobby boxes Nov 4 to 9 and $435 on the 13 sold over the weekend, against $379.99 on release day. Inception Football is one seven-card pack for $379.99 with one autograph, the NFL license back for the first time since 2015 (the 2024 set had the logos removed), and a 50-card rookie run led by Fernando Mendoza, Ty Simpson and Jeremiyah Love. The case for it is the license and the quarterback class in a brand whose rookie autographs have been the point of the product since 2015. The case against it is arithmetic: last year's box was $249.99 with two autographs and a relic, this year's is $379.99 with one autograph, and the one autograph is a plain Rookie Autograph roughly one box in four (1:4 packs) and a variation one in ten; last year those two sets settled at $6 and $16 medians across the checklist, with the top four quarterbacks carrying the price at $200 to $365 each. The odds sheet reads as about 60,000 hobby boxes if the 1/1 odds are taken at face value, which is not a small run for a one-pack product. Topps.com sold the presale at $369.99 and the release-day allotment by raffle; the first weekend answered one question: Mendoza's numbered Rookie Autographs opened at $800 (Red /99, Nov 7) and $2,000 (Gold Electricity /50, Nov 8), above where Maye's plain autograph sat a year on, while the other rookie autographs settled at $75 median on 23 sales. One name carries the box.

    Most of the pack. Inception base is a thick, numbered-feel card with a $1 to $2 market; the only grading case is a Mendoza or Simpson base RC, and last year's PSA 10 rookies at $36 against a $39.99 grading fee say no.

    The likeliest autograph in the box and, outside the top four or five names, a $5 to $15 card on sticker. This is the number that sets the value of a $380 box: one autograph with a $6 median checklist, carried by whether the name is one of the quarterbacks.

  • Launch inserts (25) and Rookie Short Prints (25, unnumbered Alpha parallels)Mendoza Rookie Short Print $255 (1, Nov 7); no Launch sold in the read. Comp: 2024 Launch about $9

    The only inserts in the set. Launch is the brand's recurring rookie insert and a $5 to $10 card; the Rookie Short Prints are the same 25 players and will depend on how short they prove once breakers post pull counts.

What to skip

  • Paying $475 for a box as a way to get a Mendoza autograph. Rookie Autographs fall 1:4 packs across 46 names, so a Mendoza is roughly one box in 180; his numbered ones opened at $800 and $2,000, so the single is not cheap either, but at least it is the card.

  • Treating this like last year's box. The 2024 product was $249.99 with two autographs and a relic and the logos removed; this one is $379.99 with one autograph and the license. The license is worth something, but not a third hit's worth.

  • Reading the FDI's $900 into the hobby box. FDI boxes carry a /22 patch autograph and a second autograph; the hobby box carries neither.

  • Buying rookie Purple /150 and Red /99 parallels at autograph prices. They are the commonest numbered cards in the set and $3 to $10 cards for everyone but the top three.

  • Trusting a two-autograph description. DraftKings Network wrote two autographs per hobby box; Checklist Insider's odds sheet and Topps' launch page say one. Price the box on one.

  • Verdict for now: a singles product, with one exception. The box sells at $475 on eBay against $379.99 at Topps, so the raffle allotment was underpriced and the secondary market is paying a $95 premium for a seven-card pack with one sticker autograph from a 46-name set whose non-Mendoza median is $75. Mendoza is the exception: $800 for a Red /99 and $2,000 for a Gold /50 in the first weekend are the Maye prices of a year on, paid in week one. Buy Simpson and Beck Rookie Autographs now while breakers list ($125 to $300); wait on Mendoza unless the Raiders start keeps going; buy the box only if a seven-card rip at $475 is the point. Investors: the second-week box median against $475 is the number to watch; the 2024 box sits near $510 sealed on a $249.99 start with three hits.
