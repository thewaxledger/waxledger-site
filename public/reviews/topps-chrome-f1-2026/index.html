2026 Topps Chrome Formula 1

4.8WAX SCORE / 10
  • Hobby: 20 packs of 4 (80 cards) with one autograph, four Prism Refractors and three or more numbered parallels. $469.99 at Topps' Sep 15 presale, $479.99 on release day; $850 median on eBay around release (10 boxes sold Oct 13 to 15), $677 two weeks on (26 boxes sold Oct 28 to Nov 1, $630 to $800) and $630 a month on (69 boxes sold Nov 1 to 14, $560 to $900; $630 on the 12 sold Nov 10 to 16, $600 to $695), down from $948 in presale
  • Mega box: 6 packs of 7, $209 median on eBay presale (21 sold Oct 3 to 12). Value box: 28 cards, $71 median (47 sold Oct 1 to 13)
  • 200-card base set: 22 F1 drivers, 22 F2, 27 F3, 17 F1 Academy, then cars, Grand Prix winners, legends, duos, team logos and team principals
  • One rookie card on the checklist: Arvid Lindblad #12 (and his car card, #100). His F1 Debut Patch Autograph is a 1/1. A month on, the #12 base is $2.60 (8 sales Nov 10 to 15) and the Refractor $5.50 to $19 (13 sales Nov 8 to 16)
  • Topps has not published pack odds or a print run. First Day Issue boxes shipped Oct 9; 18 single cards had sold on eBay by Oct 13; by Nov 17 every tier below has a read (base autographs $24 median on 42 sales Nov 15 to 17)

Written Oct 13, two days before the hobby release (Oct 15, 11am ET at Topps); hobby box price updated Oct 15 ($850 median on 10 eBay sales Oct 13 to 15). The checklist and the box contents are published; pack odds are not, so nothing below is stated as a ratio unless Topps gave a per-box or per-case count. Box prices are real presale sales from eBay sold listings. Singles re-priced Nov 17, a month after release: a base autograph is $24 (42 sales), Lindblad's rookie card is $2.60 and his Refractor $9.50, Verstappen's base is $3 and his Helmet Collection insert $35, and the hobby box is $630. The October single-card reads (18 early sales from First Day Issue boxes) are kept beside the new ones. The short version: a hobby box costs about twice what Topps charged for it, it holds 80 cards and one autograph from a list of about 100 signers that runs from Verstappen to F3 and F1 Academy drivers, and the checklist has one rookie card.

What to skip

  • Paying $630 or more for a hobby box that Topps sold for $469.99 and $479.99 on release day. The 31 single boxes sold on eBay in presale had a $935 median; the 10 sold Oct 13 to 15 were $850; a month on it is $630 (12 sold Nov 10 to 16), still $150 over Topps. That is about $11.85 a card for 80 cards and one autograph. Try the Topps sale first.

  • Reading the Molten Mercury sales as hobby prices. Every four-figure sale so far is a First Day Issue card numbered to 15.

  • Buying a hobby box for Lindblad. One rookie card in a 200-card set, with three or more numbered parallels a box spread across the base set and the inserts. Buy the single after release.

  • Assuming the autograph is a current F1 driver. The Chrome Autographs list has about 100 subjects; 22 drivers are on the grid, and the rest are F2, F3 and F1 Academy drivers, team principals and retired drivers.

  • Confusing the formats when buying sealed. Hobby (Oct 15), mega and value boxes, First Day Issue (shipped Oct 9), Hongbao, Logofractor (Oct 29) and Sapphire (Nov 12) are seven different products with different parallels.
