Written Nov 10, the day before release, with presale box prices and no 2026 singles. Museum Collection is Topps' mid-high-end baseball box: eight cards, one on-card autograph, one autograph relic and one relic, $409.99 at the Topps presale and $600 median on eBay presale (18 single boxes Oct 27 to Nov 9), the same format as last year's box, which still sells sealed at $672 (7 sales since September). The case for it is the checklist: a 100-card base set whose 28 rookies include both Rookie of the Year winners, McGonigle and Stewart, plus Anthony, Griffin, Murakami, Wetherholt and Caglianone, and 769 autograph cards with the framed autographs and the Canvas Collection originals at the top. The case against it is what the three hits usually are. Cardsmiths Breaks' odds put an Archival Autograph (72 cards, sticker, $14 median last year) in three boxes of four and a Signature Swatches dual relic autograph in one of two; the framed autographs that carry the brand ($112 median last year, $400 to $760 for Griffey, Jackson and Ryan) fall about once a case, and the Canvas originals once in 556 cases. At $600 the box needs a framed autograph or a top-rookie patch autograph to pay; at $409.99 from Topps it needs less. The number to watch is the first-week eBay median against the 2025 box's $672: if the 2026 box opens above it on the strength of the rookie class, the sealed market is pricing the names in before any of them has sold.

Tier 1 · The cards worth opening a box for

  • Museum Framed Autographs (74 cards: Silver, Bronze, Gold and Black frames, /35 down to /5) and Framed Hall of Fame Autographs (2)2026 prices pending. Comp: 2025 framed autographs $112 median on 24 sales Oct 27 to Nov 9; Griffey Gold Frame /20 $760, Bo Jackson Bronze /20 $395, Nolan Ryan /20 $229, Imanaga Black /5 $90, Hernandez Bronze /35 $70, Murray Silver /25 $47, Larkin /25 $33

    The card the brand is known for: a signed, framed, serial-numbered card that falls about once a case (Cardsmiths Breaks). The name decides the price: Griffey's Gold Frame /20 went $760 last year and a current player's Silver /25 $15 to $50. Buy the single of the player you want rather than the case.

  • Roman Anthony, Konnor Griffin, Kevin McGonigle, Munetaka Murakami · Archival Autographs, Signature Swatches, Momentous Material Jumbo Patch Autographs, framed autographs and the numbered basePrices pending. Comp: Anthony 2026 Topps #189 base is $40 in PSA 10 (158 sales in 90 days, this site's Raw-to-Gem board); 2025 rookie Archival Autographs in the read were $1 to $12 (Wrobleski Gold /149 $12, Lee Pink Sapphire /99 $11.73, Festa /25 $1.25 to $3.25)

    The four names the 2026 rookie class is built on, and the first Museum autographs for all of them. McGonigle's AL Rookie of the Year (Nov 9) lands two days before release; Anthony's finger is the only injury story. The plain Archival Autographs will set the floor; the framed and jumbo patch versions carry the box's upside.

  • Canvas Collection Original Player Autographs (10, 1 in 556 cases), Atelier Autographed Book Cards (31, 1 in 3 cases), Dual On-Card Autographs (8, 1 in 32 cases)Prices pending. No 2025 Canvas original sold in the Oct 27 to Nov 9 read

    The case hits. The Canvas originals are one-of-one hand-painted cards that sell one at a time; the Atelier books are the autographed book cards. Neither belongs in a box-value calculation at 1 in 3 cases or longer.

  • Lifetime Members Relic Autographs (34, 1 in 4 cases) and Showpieces (42, about 1 a case)Prices pending. Comp: 2025 patch autographs $51 median on 23 sales (Contreras Momentous /15 $78, McCutchen /20 $76, Altuve Gold /15 $51)

    The milestone-club autograph relics (the 3,000-hit club, per Checklist Insider) and the big-swatch Showpieces are the second tier of the autograph checklist; retired names in both, so the price is the name, not the rookie market.

Tier 3 · Everything else in the pack

  • Base cards · 100, about 2.7 a boxPrices pending. Comp: 2025 base $2.54 median on 22 sales (Nolan Ryan $3.99, Jeter $0.99, Sasaki RC $2.49)

    Two or three thick base cards a box with a $1 to $4 market. A rookie base (Anthony, Griffin, McGonigle) is the only one with a reason to grade, and Anthony's cheaper 2026 Topps base at $40 in PSA 10 says the Museum base will not clear a $39.99 fee either.

  • Archival Autographs · 72 cards, 3 in 4 boxesPrices pending. Comp: 2025 Archival Autographs $13.69 median on 53 sales Oct 27 to Nov 9 ($0.99 to $249); Canseco /199 $26, Wrobleski Gold /149 $12, Festa and Matthews /25 $1.25 to $3.90

    The autograph most boxes contain and the reason the box's floor is low: a sticker autograph from a 72-name set whose median was $14 a year on, with the rookies and retired stars the exceptions. This is the number that sets the value of a $410 to $600 box.

  • Meaningful Material Relics (74, 1 in 2.2 boxes), Museum Memorabilia I and II (98 and 92, 1 in 6.2 each), Primary Pieces multi-relicsPrices pending. Comp: 2025 relics $20 median on 49 sales (De La Cruz Primary quad /49 $43, Henderson/Jackson dual quad /75 $55, Correa Meaningful /99 $2.28)

    The guaranteed relic. Single-swatch Meaningful Material cards are $2 to $12; the quad and jumbo versions of young stars reach $40 to $55. Topps does not state game use per card.

What to skip

  • Paying $600 on eBay for a box Topps sells at $409.99 and expecting the hits to cover it. Three of four boxes carry an Archival Autograph with a $14 median and one of two a Signature Swatches sticker autograph; the framed autograph is a once-a-case card.

  • Treating Cardsmiths Breaks' odds as Topps'. Topps has not published pack odds for this set; the per-box and per-case figures above are Cardsmiths' estimates and the parallel print runs differ between sources (Gold /275 or /249).

  • Buying the 2025 box as a comp for the hits. It holds $672 sealed on a $350 to $400 start because sealed Museum boxes keep their price, not because the average box returns it; last year's singles medians are $14 (Archival), $51 (patch autographs) and $20 (relics).

  • Grading Museum base rookies. A thick $2 to $4 card whose PSA 10 will sit near the $40 of Anthony's flagship base does not clear the $39.99 fee.

  • Verdict for now: a box to buy at Topps' price and a singles product at eBay's. $409.99 for three hits from this checklist is the fairest ask of Topps' fall slate; $600 on eBay before any 2026 card has sold is paying for the rookie names in advance. Investors: Museum boxes hold sealed (the 2025 box is $672 on a $350 to $400 start), so a Topps-price box kept sealed has a better record than a ripped one; the first-week median against $672 is the number.
