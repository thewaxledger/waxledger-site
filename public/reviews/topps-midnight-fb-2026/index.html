Written Nov 12, the day before release (Nov 13), from presale box sales and the handful of FDI singles that have sold; every hobby-box single price is pending. Midnight Football is one seven-card pack for $539.99 with three autographs, the NFL license for the first time (the 2024 debut was airbrushed and the brand skipped 2025), and a 37-rookie base run led by Fernando Mendoza, Ty Simpson and Jeremiyah Love. The market has already priced it: 43 presale boxes sold on eBay between Oct 12 and Nov 11 at a $680 median, $140 over Topps, and the first week of flips went $720. The case for the box is three autographs in seven cards, which is three times what Inception gives for $160 more, and a 2024 box that went from $249.99 to $900-plus asking in 17 months. The case against it is that the 2024 box was a $250 product when it did that; this one starts at $540 and trades at $680, and the three autographs come from eleven sets totalling 336 cards, most of them $5 to $60 names on last year's evidence (2024 Rookie Horizon Signatures and Stroke of Midnight sell at $2.50 to $12 a year on; Nabers $60; the retired greats $17 to $195). The FDI singles that have sold say the same: $250 for a Harrison /10 and $300 for a Kittle /35 at the top, $40 and $60 for rookie autographs below the first tier. Whether Mendoza's three autograph sets open where his Inception ones did ($800 Red /99, $2,000 Gold /50 in week one) is the number to fill in next week.

Tier 1 · The cards worth opening a box for

  • Fernando Mendoza #21 RC · Base Autograph, Rookie Horizon Signatures, Rookie Jersey Autograph, and the numbered coloursPrices pending; no Mendoza single sold through Nov 11. Comp: his Inception Rookie Autograph Red /99 $800 (Nov 7) and Gold Electricity /50 $2,000 (Nov 8), from this site's Inception review

    The first pick and the card the box is bought for, in a set where he appears in three autograph checklists. His Inception numbered autographs opened at Maye-a-year-on prices in week one; three Midnight sets means three times the supply of his signature, which is the one reason to expect the plain ones to open lower. The Raiders start decides the second month.

  • Ty Simpson #61, Jeremiyah Love #15, Carson Beck #27 · rookie autographsPrices pending. Comp: Simpson Inception autographs $150 to $300 (6 sales Nov 6 to 9); Beck $125 and $190

    The second tier of a quarterback-heavy class plus the one running back with a market. Inception's first weekend put Simpson at $150 to $300 and Beck at $125 to $190; expect Midnight to track those, with the Moonrise /25 and shorter colours the ones that hold.

  • Constellations · 15 dual-player cards (Barry Sanders / Jahmyr Gibbs previewed)Sanders / Gibbs $99.99 (1 sale, Nov 7, FDI)

    The one insert with a three-figure sale so far. Fifteen pairings of a legend and a current player; the single is cheaper than a box and the only way to pick the pairing.

  • Stroke of Midnight (15) and Horizon Signatures (19) · the veteran and retired-great autographs, Winter Solstice /35 and shorterKittle Stroke of Midnight /35 $300 (1 sale, Nov 8, FDI); Harrison Jr. autograph /10 $250 (Nov 10). Comp: 2024 Horizon Signatures a year on: Brees Twilight /99 $195, Polamalu $125.50, Aikman /35 $89, down to Long, Kosar and Manley at $3 to $18

    The autographs that hold: short-numbered veterans and Hall of Famers. The 2024 set shows the split, with Brees, Polamalu and Aikman at $89 to $195 and the rest of the checklist at $3 to $30 a year later. Buy the name, not the set.

    A jersey swatch with a sticker autograph; the 2024 version is a $12 card for a first-round pass rusher a year on. Sadiq at $100 is a Jets tight end in week one of FDI supply, not a settled price.

  • Dark Matter (29), Midnight Oil Marks (28), Countdown Calligraphy (37), Tenebrous Traces (20), REM Relic Autographs (23)Prices pending

    Five autograph sets that make up most of the 336 autograph cards and most of the three-a-box. Dark Matter and Midnight Oil Marks are the two sets brought over from the NBA release; the rest are the filler that sets the box's floor. No odds sheet yet, so which set falls in which box is a breaker's question until the first cases are opened.

  • Numbered base parallels · Morning /125, Twilight /99, Dusk /75, Summer Solstice /50, Winter Solstice /35, Moonrise /25, Equinox /20, Midnight /12, Daybreak /5, Witching Hour /3Prices pending

    Thirteen tiers on a 100-card set with no odds published; the FDI boxes carry two parallels, so the hobby box likely carries one or two of the seven. Rookie Moonrise /25 and shorter are the only base parallels worth more than a grading fee on the 2024 evidence.

  • Case hits · Celestial Roots, Dream Land, Night Night, Twilight (25 each, Black Light 1/1 parallels)Prices pending

    Four short-printed insert sets at about one a case, two of them new. No sale yet; a Mendoza or Mahomes in any of the four is the card to chase as a single once breakers post pull counts.

Tier 3 · Everything else in the pack

  • Base cards · one or two of the seven, 63 veterans and 37 rookies, Zodiac and Moon Beam unnumbered parallelsJosh Cameron #45 RC $9.99 (1 sale, Nov 6, FDI); no other base sale read

    The least of the pack: with three autographs and the parallels, the base card is the one that is left over. A $10 sale for a Cameron rookie out of an FDI box is first-week scarcity, not a price; this is not a grading product.

  • Rookie Horizon Signatures (35) and Base Autographs (75) · the commonest autographsNussmeier Base Autograph $60 (Nov 8, FDI); Heidenreich Rookie Horizon Signatures $39.99 (Nov 9, FDI). Comp: 2024 Rookie Horizon Signatures a year on: Nubin $5, Wright /75 $5.99, McCaffrey $7.99, Rice Moon Beam $5, Nabers $60 (Sep 28)

    The likeliest autographs in the box and, outside the top five or six names, $5 to $15 cards on the 2024 evidence. Three of these a box is the arithmetic that has to clear $680: at last year's medians it does not, and the box's value is whether one of the three is a Mendoza, a Simpson or a short colour.

  • Nightball (30), Insomnia (30), Earthshine (25) · the unnumbered inserts and their /125 coloursChase Nightball Morning /125 $29.99 and Maye Nightball Morning /125 $29.99 (Nov 7, FDI)

    The inserts that fill the non-autograph slots. The /125 colours of the two biggest names sold at $30 in week one of FDI supply; the unnumbered versions will be $3 to $8 cards once hobby boxes open.

What to skip

  • Paying $680 for a box as a way to get a Mendoza autograph. He is in three autograph sets of 35 to 75 cards each with no odds published; the single will cost less than the box in week one, as his Inception autographs did (which were still $800 and $2,000 for the numbered ones).

  • Reading the 2024 box's $900-plus asking into this one. The 2024 box was $249.99 and unlicensed; the climb to $900 happened from a quarter of this year's price, and those are asking prices, not sales.

  • Reading the FDI's $1,500 into the hobby box. FDI boxes carry a Molten Mercury autograph /10 and two parallels; the hobby box carries neither, and the $1,400 to $1,850 FDI sales are six boxes.

  • Buying Rookie Horizon Signatures and Base Autographs of second-tier names at the first-week price. The 2024 versions settled at $5 to $12 a year on for everyone but Nabers.

  • Treating presale flips at $720 as the street price. The first-week median was $720 and the November median is $680; a one-pack product with three autographs usually dips once breakers post the first cases.

  • Verdict for now: a singles product with a better box than Inception. Three autographs at $540 is a fairer rip than one at $380, and the 2024 box's climb shows what a licensed Midnight can do, but that climb began at $250 and this one begins at $540 with a $680 street price already on it. Buy Simpson, Beck and Love autographs in the first week while breakers list; wait on Mendoza unless the Raiders start keeps going; buy the box at Topps' $540 if you can get it and only at $680 if a seven-card rip with three autographs is the point. Investors: the second-week box median against $680 is the number to watch, and whether it holds above $600 once the first cases are broken.
