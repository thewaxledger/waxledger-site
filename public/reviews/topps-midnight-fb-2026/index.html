Written Nov 12, re-priced Nov 14 from the first two days of hobby-box sales (release Nov 13). Midnight Football is one seven-card pack for $539.99 with three autographs, the NFL license for the first time (the 2024 debut was airbrushed and the brand skipped 2025), and a 37-rookie base run led by Fernando Mendoza, Ty Simpson and Jeremiyah Love. The market priced it at $680 in presale (43 boxes Oct 12 to Nov 11) and $720 in the first week of flips; on release the box slipped to $650 median on 11 sales Nov 13 to 14 ($580 to $675), still $110 over Topps. The case for the box is three autographs in seven cards, which is three times what Inception gives for $160 more, and a 2024 box that went from $249.99 to $900-plus asking in 17 months. The case against it is that the 2024 box was a $250 product when it did that; this one starts at $540 and trades at $680, and the three autographs come from eleven sets totalling 336 cards, most of them $5 to $60 names on last year's evidence (2024 Rookie Horizon Signatures and Stroke of Midnight sell at $2.50 to $12 a year on; Nabers $60; the retired greats $17 to $195). The first two days of hobby singles say the same: 40 autographs sold at a $100 median, and the ones above $200 were short-numbered (Allar Rookie Jersey Autograph /12 $699, Sanders Dark Matter /20 $500, Tate REM /20 $300) or Caleb Downs and Jeremiyah Love; Reese, Johnson, Concepcion and Beck autographs run $75 to $175, the veterans' Countdown Calligraphy $20 to $100. No Mendoza autograph has sold from a hobby box yet; his Equinox autograph /20 went $950 on Nov 13 and his Rookie Refractor SP #21 $103, so whether his base autographs open where his Inception ones did ($800 Red /99, $2,000 Gold /50 in week one) is still the number to fill in.

What to skip

  • Paying $680 for a box as a way to get a Mendoza autograph. He is in three autograph sets of 35 to 75 cards each with no odds published; the single will cost less than the box in week one, as his Inception autographs did (which were still $800 and $2,000 for the numbered ones).

  • Reading the 2024 box's $900-plus asking into this one. The 2024 box was $249.99 and unlicensed; the climb to $900 happened from a quarter of this year's price, and those are asking prices, not sales.

  • Reading the FDI's $1,500 into the hobby box. FDI boxes carry a Molten Mercury autograph /10 and two parallels; the hobby box carries neither, and the $1,400 to $1,850 FDI sales are six boxes.

  • Buying Rookie Horizon Signatures and Base Autographs of second-tier names at the first-week price. The 2024 versions settled at $5 to $12 a year on for everyone but Nabers.

  • Treating presale flips at $720 as the street price. The first-week median was $720 and the November median is $680; a one-pack product with three autographs usually dips once breakers post the first cases.

  • Verdict for now: a singles product with a better box than Inception. Three autographs at $540 is a fairer rip than one at $380, and the 2024 box's climb shows what a licensed Midnight can do, but that climb began at $250 and this one begins at $540 with a $680 street price already on it. Buy Simpson, Beck and Love autographs in the first week while breakers list; wait on Mendoza unless the Raiders start keeps going; buy the box at Topps' $540 if you can get it and only at $680 if a seven-card rip with three autographs is the point. Investors: the second-week box median against $680 is the number to watch, and whether it holds above $600 once the first cases are broken.
