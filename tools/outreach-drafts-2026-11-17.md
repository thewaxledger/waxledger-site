## r/footballcardz (post Tue Nov 17 evening or Wed morning, before Chrome Football hits shelves)

**Title:** Topps Chrome Football drops tomorrow. Before you rip, here's which 2023-2024 QB Prizm Silvers actually pay to grade (PSA sales, real gem rates, eBay raw)

I re-ran the grading math this morning on the QB rookie Silvers everyone has in a box somewhere.

**Method.** All-in = raw (eBay sold median, last 45 days) + PSA fee ($39.99 Value if the 10 is under $500, $59.99 Standard above) + $10 shipping. A 10 sells at 80% of its 90-day PSA median, a 9 at 85%, both minus a 13% selling fee. Weighted by the card's gem rate from the PSA pop (10s / everything graded).

| Card | Raw | PSA 9 | PSA 10 | PSA 10 sales, 90 days | Gem rate | All-in | Expected profit per card |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caleb Williams 2024 Prizm #301 Silver | $225 | $306 | $1,925 | 115 | 18% | $295 | +$135 |
| Bryce Young 2023 Prizm #311 Silver | $46 | $56 | $311 | 191 | 46% | $96 | +$26 |
| Drake Maye 2024 Prizm #329 Silver | $135 | $145 | $985 | 93 | 21% | $205 | +$23 |
| Bo Nix 2024 Prizm #309 Silver | $55 | $76 | $551 | 88 | 22% | $125 | +$4 |
| Michael Penix Jr. 2024 Prizm #378 Silver | $21 | $45 | $271 | 96 | 22% | $71 | –$4 |
| J.J. McCarthy 2024 Prizm #400 Silver | $26 | $39 | $250 | 150 | 26% | $76 | –$10 |
| Jayden Daniels 2024 Prizm #347 Silver | $67 | $80 | $719 | 53 | 12% | $137 | –$25 |
| Trevor Lawrence 2021 Prizm #331 base | $4 | $20 | $160 | 19 | 9% | $54 | –$30 |

What stood out:

- **Caleb is the only one with real room.** The 10 went from about $1,600 to $1,925 in a month and raw only moved $25. A 9 still loses about $70, so it's a bet on the 18%.
- **Daniels is the trap.** $719 ten, but only 12% of everything PSA has graded is a 10, and 38% is an 8 or lower.
- **Maye and Nix are break-even with extra steps.** Both clear on paper but by $23 and $4, so the raw price you pay decides it.
- **Penix and McCarthy Silvers have the volume (96 and 150 sales) but not the price.** A $250 ten after the haircut and fees doesn't pay for the misses.

Not advice, just arithmetic. Happy to run any card people are thinking about sending in.

- **The refractors are where the gap is.** Raw refractors run $14 to $80 for everyone but Flagg, and the 10s run $285 to $1,314.

- **Harper base is the one base card with real room,** because his 9 ($49) is ten times the raw price.
- Look hard at centring before you buy raw refractors; a lot of what's listed has probably been passed over already.

Not advice, just arithmetic.
